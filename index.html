Common questions before you email.

Answers for creators searching how to get sponsors on YouTube and sponsors looking for creators with measurable audience proof.

What metrics should creators send?

Send three clear groups: followers/subscribers, view count and engagement. Screenshots can include YouTube Studio overview, recent video views, average views, top videos, likes, comments, watch time, audience location, retention, posting frequency and past sponsor videos.

What creator levels can you review?

We can review creators at 1k–9k, 10k–99k, 100k–999k and 1m+ followers or subscribers. The level helps organise creators, but sponsor fit depends on view count, engagement, niche, audience trust and brand safety too.

Can creators with 1k to 9k followers apply?

Yes. Smaller creators can be valuable when they have a specific niche, strong trust and clear engagement. They may fit smaller, local, niche or community sponsor campaigns.

Do I need to already have sponsors?

No, but existing sponsor experience helps. If you have already done sponsor videos, brand integrations, affiliate deals or gifted collaborations, include proof because it can make your pitch stronger.

What does pay nothing until a deal is completed mean?

Creators can send their information for review without an upfront application payment. The current early success fee is 2% of profits from a completed sponsor deal. This percentage is likely to increase in the future and terms are confirmed in writing before any deal.

Can sponsors tell you their exact creator requirements?

Yes. Sponsors should send target niche, creator level, minimum followers, expected view count, engagement signals, audience location, budget range, campaign goal and permission to be contacted about future creator matches.

Is there a guarantee I will get a sponsor?

No. This is an honest new business. Review does not guarantee a sponsorship deal, and the site does not use fake testimonials, fake results or invented case studies. The aim is to use real metrics to create better sponsor conversations.

Can I communicate by WhatsApp instead of email?

Yes. Email is fine, a call is fine, and WhatsApp is fine. Include your phone number only if you are comfortable being contacted that way.
