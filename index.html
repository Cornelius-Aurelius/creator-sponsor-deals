Common sponsorship questions.

Short answers for creators and sponsors deciding whether to send details.

How do I get sponsors for my YouTube channel?

Send your YouTube channel URL, username, email and screenshots proving your followers, views and engagement. Sponsors need proof that your audience is real, engaged and relevant.

What screenshots should creators send?

Send screenshots of followers or subscribers, recent views, average views, top videos, likes, comments, watch time, retention, audience location and past sponsor content if available.

Do you care about future data?

No. First review is based on already proven data from your channel. Do not send promises about future views as proof.

Do creators pay upfront?

No upfront creator application fee. The current early success fee is 2% of profit after a completed sponsor deal. This may increase later. Terms are confirmed before any deal.

How long can it take?

Please allow up to 45 days for review and sponsor fit. A review does not guarantee a sponsor deal.

Can small creators apply?

Yes. Creator levels start from 1k–9k followers or subscribers, but stronger view count, engagement and niche fit will matter.

What should sponsors send?

Sponsors should send brand name, website, target niche, campaign goal, creator level, minimum views, engagement requirements, target audience and permission to be contacted about future creator matches.

Is this a new business?

Yes. This is a brand-new business, so the site does not use fake testimonials, fake case studies or invented results. The first step is simple: send real data and confirm interest.
