Common sponsorship questions.

Answers for YouTube creators and sponsors searching for YouTube sponsor deals, paid YouTube brand deals and sponsor matching.

How do I apply?

Click the creator Gmail button, complete the email template inside Gmail, attach screenshots of your YouTube metrics, then send it to 138licensing@gmail.com.

What screenshots should creators attach?

Attach screenshots of YouTube subscribers, recent views, average views, likes, comments, watch time, audience location, retention, subscriber growth and past sponsor videos if available.

Do you use future data?

No. The first review only cares about proven YouTube data that already exists. Do not send guesses, predictions or promises instead of screenshots. Screenshots may still be checked again before anything is finalised.

Do I need 100k YouTube subscribers?

No. The site reviews 1k–9k, 10k–99k, 100k–999k and 1m+ YouTube subscriber levels. Smaller niche YouTube creators may still be useful when engagement is strong.

Do creators pay upfront?

No upfront creator application fee. The current early success fee is 2% after a completed sponsor deal. The percentage is likely to increase in the future. Terms should be confirmed in writing before any deal.

How long should I wait?

Please allow up to 45 days for review, sponsor fit and possible follow-up. Submitting data does not guarantee a sponsorship deal.

What happens before anything is finalised?

Before any sponsor deal is finalised, both sides are checked again. Creator screenshots, YouTube channel data, sponsor details, campaign requirements and written terms are verified for accuracy.

Can I speak to someone first?

Yes. You can use email only, ask for a call, or include a phone number and say WhatsApp is okay. There is no pressure to communicate one specific way.

What should sponsors send?

Sponsors should send brand name, website, contact email, target YouTube subscriber level, niche, target audience, campaign goal, budget range if available, preferred YouTube content type, required creator metrics and permission to be contacted about future matches.

Is there a guarantee?

No. This is a brand-new business. Submitting details starts a review and matching process; it does not guarantee a deal.
